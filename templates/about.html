About the Project

Problem: Companies need to choose stock items that maximize profit while remaining within budget and storage limits.

Objective: Provide a practical demonstration of the 0/1 Knapsack algorithm using a clean web-based interface.

Algorithm: Dynamic Programming helps evaluate combinations efficiently and avoid brute-force search.

Working: Each product contributes to cost, storage, and profit. The system selects the combination that produces the highest profit under constraints.

Advantages: Fast decision support, useful academic demonstration, and simple UI for practical understanding.

Future Scope: Add real-time inventory integration, multi-warehouse planning, and more advanced optimization models.
