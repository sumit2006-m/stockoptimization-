Stock Purchase & Storage Cost Optimization System

This system demonstrates the 0/1 Knapsack Algorithm using dynamic programming to choose the most profitable stock combinations under budget and storage constraints.

Start Optimization Manage Products

Problem Statement

Organizations must choose stock purchases within limited budget and storage resources.

Objectives

Maximize total profit while ensuring cost and storage constraints are respected.

Algorithm Used

Dynamic Programming based 0/1 Knapsack to compute the optimal selection.
